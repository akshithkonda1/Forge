## Why This License?
Forge exists to solve a real problem: health data is fragmented across dozens of apps and devices, and users deserve coherent, personalized, trustworthy intelligence about their own bodies from products that don't typically make it easier to get an idea of what one's health is really like. 

Building that well is extremely difficult, and using new technologies such as Generative AI, Machine Learning, and large data modules makes it harder. 
It requires deep integration across the fitness technology ecosystem we're part of. 
Wearables, AI reasoning, privacy engineering, and delightful interfaces are just some of the things we need to do right to ensure Forge is safe for public use.

We believe in transparency, but we also believe in the safe deployment and use of our product.
You can see that in how Forge approaches unified health intelligence. 
But the actual implementation — the careful orchestration of data, the nuanced coaching logic, the production-grade architecture — represents focused work, lots of time and a dedication to the craft. 
We protect it so fiercely because, done wrong, it can have horrible consequences to our users, people though we may never meet, still care very deeply about.
But also so that high-quality health AI remains something worth building properly, not something cheaply cloned to make a copy and to protect what we've here at Forge have made.

If you want to build something in this space, we encourage you to do it from first principles. If you want to use Forge's actual technology, let's talk.
